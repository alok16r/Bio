About Me

A bit about what I do

Started editing with screen recordings on XRecorder, then moved through KineMaster, Vita, and CapCut. Today, I work in DaVinci Resolve. After 500+ videos and 5M+ views, I'm still creating, learning, and pushing forward.

Sketching is the quieter side. When something catches my eye, I put it on paper. Two different kinds of creativity, I love both equally.
